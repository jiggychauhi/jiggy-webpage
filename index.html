Research

Qi-Wu-Zhang (QWZ) Model in the Strong-Coupling Limit

We are currently exploring the QWZ model with spin-selective interactions in the strong-coupling limit and trying to build the effective hamiltonains for soliton solutions.

Quantum Kernel SVM for Parkinson's Disease Classification

We explored Parkinson's disease classification from gait video recordings by representing them with covariance descriptors on Riemannian manifolds and training support vector machines. Alongside the classical RBF kernel, we benchmarked several quantum kernels, including Z, ZZ, and custom covariance-based feature maps. By combining these quantum-enhanced kernels with common spatial pattern filtering, we highlight how quantum approaches can enrich machine learning pipelines for studying neurodegenerative diseases.

[Quantum Mach. Intell. 7, 64 (2025)]

Quantum Denoising for BCI

We conducted research on passive Brain-Computer Interfaces using EEG data, focusing on self-supervised learning and the application of quantum autoencoders for signal denoising. Our study demonstrated the technical feasibility of quantum autoencoding with real EEG time-series data and opened new avenues for exploring quantum approaches in neuroscience and brain-computer interfaces.

Course Projects

Uderstanding Algorithmic Learning Through Transformer Shortcuts

We reviewed the paper "Transformers Learn Shortcuts to Automata" (Liu et al., 2022), which explores how Transformers handle sequential logic without recurrence. The work shows that instead of step by step processing, they often rely on “shortcut solutions” that compress entire sequences into parallel layers, an approach that is efficient but limited in generalization.

A Quantum Approach to Option Pricing

We reviewed and reproduced the results of the paper "Option Pricing Using Quantum Computers" (Stamatopoulos et al., 2020), which applies quantum amplitude estimation to option pricing. We replicated the circuits and methodology, analyzed extensions for practical implementation on current quantum hardware, and reflected on the challenges and limitations of applying quantum algorithms to real-world financial problems.
